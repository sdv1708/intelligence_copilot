3. KEY_TOPICS_TODAY
   - The handful of things that genuinely need discussion this meeting.

4. PROPOSED_AGENDA
   - Sequence the discussion to maximise value: quick decisions first, then the
     items that need real deliberation.
   - Each entry names the topic *and* the outcome it should reach, plus a
     realistic time box in minutes and an owner where one is known.
   - Leave room for decisions, not just status updates.

   - The specific facts, figures, and quotes underpinning the brief above.
   - `source` must be copied verbatim from a `Source:` line in the context.
   - `snippet` is the relevant excerpt itself, not your paraphrase of it.
